Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and ethical use of digital technologies such as the internet, social media, computers, and mobile devices.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the rules, regulations, and legal rights that govern the use of technology and digital information.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. An Acceptable Use Policy is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use a computer system, network, internet service, or digital resources.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

Examples of plagiarism include: Copying text from a website and pasting it into an assignment without citing the source. Turning in someone else's work as your own. Using another person's ideas without giving credit. Copying and slightly changing words while keeping the original idea without a citation.

What is Acedemic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair academic advantage or misrepresents their own work.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI-generated content should only be used according to your school or organization's rules, and you should be honest about using AI when required.
