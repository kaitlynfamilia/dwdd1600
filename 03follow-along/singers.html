Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include:

Thomas Jefferson
Thomas Jefferson was an American Founding Father and the third President of the United States from 1801 to 1809. He is best known for writing and signing the Declaration of Independence in 1776, which announced the American colonies' freedom from Great Britain. Jefferson also completed the Louisiana Purchase in 1803, greatly expanding the size of the United States.
Jhon Adams
John Adams was an American Founding Father and the second President of the United States from 1797 to 1801. He played a key role in the American Revolution and helped persuade Congress to support independence from Great Britain. Adams also signed the Declaration of Independence and was one of the nation's most influential early leaders.
